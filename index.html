About Me

I am a Master's researcher in Product Design at Central South University, expecting to graduate in June 2027. My interests lie at the intersection of Human–Computer Interaction (HCI), User Experience, and Interaction Design.

I explore how people interact with technology and how these interactions shape human experiences. I am particularly interested in diverse users and specific or complex contexts, with a focus on children and users with specific needs. Through design, I hope to create interactions that are more inclusive, meaningful, and supportive of human well-being.

My work combines qualitative and quantitative research with design practice. I use interviews, observation, questionnaires, and data analysis to understand people and their interactions with technology, then translate these insights into interactive concepts and prototypes. My projects have explored contexts including education and learning, AI, AR, wearable interaction, and intelligent mobility, while maintaining a focus on human–technology interaction and experience.

Ph.D. Goal

I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities for Autumn 2027 in HCI, Human-Centered AI, or Interaction Design. I hope to further explore human–technology interaction and user experience in real-world contexts, and contribute to more meaningful and inclusive technological experiences.

Life

When I'm away from my desk, I enjoy high-altitude hiking.

I've explored trails including Zilong Secret Land, Kula Kangri, Yubeng, Ganheba, and Abuji Cuo, reaching 5,200 meters with 100+ km of cumulative distance and 10,000+ meters of elevation gain. These journeys have taught me patience, resilience, and the value of moving forward one step at a time.

I hike for the journey as much as the destination—the changing landscapes, unexpected details, and discoveries along the way.

Always curious. Always exploring.
